About Photo2Reel

Photo2Reel is a browser-based tool for turning selected photos and optional audio into a downloadable slideshow video.

Created and maintained by an independent developer.

The tool lets you arrange photos, set their timing, choose a landscape or vertical frame, and create a video in a compatible browser.

Your selected photos and audio are processed locally in the browser. Photo2Reel does not require an account or provide a server-side media upload feature.

For support, feedback, or privacy questions, visit the contact page.
