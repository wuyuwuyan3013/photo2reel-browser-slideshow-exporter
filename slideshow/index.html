How to make a video from photos with music

This video from photos tool turns a group of pictures into one downloadable slideshow video without sending your files to a server. Choose your images, give each one the time it deserves, pick a small built-in soundtrack or add your own audio, and create the video directly on your device. It is made for birthday albums, travel recaps, product photos, family memories, and short social posts that need a calmer, more personal rhythm than a single still image.

Make a photo video in three quick steps

Start by selecting several photos from your phone or computer. The order in the list is the order used in the final video, and every photo has its own duration field. A fast sequence can use two seconds per image. For a portrait, a detailed product photograph, or a sentimental moment, three to five seconds gives the viewer more room to look. Remove anything that does not fit before you render.

Next, decide whether the slideshow needs sound. You can leave it quiet, choose a gentle browser-generated background track, or upload an audio file you already own. The volume control is useful when the images are intended to sit under spoken narration later. Audio is mixed inside the page while the video is recorded; it is not submitted to a music service or stored remotely.

Finally, choose a 16:9 landscape or 9:16 vertical frame and press Create video. The tool renders the canvas with the selected soundtrack, then converts the local recording to MP4 in your browser. Once rendering is complete, the Download MP4 button appears. Processing speed depends on your device and the number and size of the photos.

Why a browser-only slideshow maker is useful

Many photo-to-video services begin with an upload. That can be convenient, yet it also means personal images travel to someone else’s infrastructure before you can see a result. This page follows a different route. The selected files are read by the browser, held in its working memory, and used to create the downloadable clip there. Closing or refreshing the page clears the temporary project. There is no login, gallery, cloud folder, or hidden publishing step.

That local approach also makes this a genuinely free video from photos workflow. There is no account to create, no credit balance, no export fee, and no watermark placed across your photos. Your device does the rendering work, so performance depends on the phone or computer and on the number and size of the files. For a long album, try a smaller batch first. It gives you a chance to check the pace and keeps the final download manageable.

Tips for a smoother result

Use photos with a similar orientation when possible. Wide images naturally fill a landscape video; tall images are placed neatly over a dark background instead of being stretched. Give the opening image an extra second, then use a steady duration for the middle of the sequence. If the music has a clear ending, adjust the last image so the visual and audio finish together. The preview reflects the same framing used for the export, so you can inspect the sequence before making the final file.
